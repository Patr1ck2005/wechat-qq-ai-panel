Dependencies are installed separately with their license notices intact. No upstream WeChat/QQ binaries, models, credentials or runtime data are included in this repository. The companion access repository maintains the message-reader versions and licenses; its NapCat dependency has non-commercial and redistribution restrictions. A MIT license on this independent panel does not change those terms.

Existing products evaluated: wx-assist and wechat-daily-report. They informed the scope comparison; their reading engines and application source were not copied into this panel.
